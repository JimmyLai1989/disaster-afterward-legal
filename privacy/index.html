1. Scope and operator

JimmyLai1989 (“we,” “us,” or “our”) publishes Disaster Afterward (the “Game”) and this legal website. This Policy applies to the Game, support we provide, and these legal pages. Services independently provided by Apple, Google, Unity, or GitHub are also governed by those providers’ policies.

2. Information the Game processes

2.1 Platform sign-in

The Game does not offer a guest mode. On a supported device, sign-in uses Apple Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android. The Game may process the platform type, a platform player identifier, display name, authentication status, sign-in time, and sign-in error information. A one-way hash of the platform identifier may be created on your device as a stable game identifier.

This information is used to authenticate access and associate local game state with the signed-in platform identity. We do not receive your Apple ID password, Google Account password, or complete platform credentials. The current release does not send this identity to a developer-operated player database.

2.2 Gameplay and local save data

The Game stores progress, settings, inventory, roster information, entitlement status, and other information needed to run the Game on your device. The current release uses local saves and does not provide a developer-operated cloud-save service.

Your operating system may include application data in a device or system backup according to your settings. Such backups are controlled by Apple or Google, not by us.

2.3 In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The current Game implementation may process a product identifier, localized product information, purchase status, transaction or order information, a receipt or purchase token, and the resulting entitlement status on your device. The currently configured product is a one-time, non-consumable premium unlock.

Apple or Google processes the payment and refund. We do not receive your full card number or bank-account credentials. The current release does not operate a separate server for receipt storage or validation.

2.4 Analytics, diagnostics, and advertising

The current release uses a disabled, no-operation analytics implementation. It does not intentionally send gameplay events to us or to an external analytics provider, and it does not integrate third-party advertising. We do not use an advertising identifier for tracking and do not sell personal information.

Apple, Google, the app store, the operating system, or their platform services may independently collect performance, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under your settings and their own policies. Those platform-level activities are not controlled by us.

2.5 Support communications

If you contact us, we process the information you choose to provide, such as the issue description, reply method, screenshots, device or Game information, and response history, to answer and resolve the request.

Our current contact channel is a public GitHub Issues page. Do not post passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, private receipts, or other sensitive information there. You may open a minimal issue asking us to arrange an appropriate follow-up channel.

2.6 Visits to this website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser information, access times, and routine technical logs needed to secure and deliver the site. We do not add cookies, local-storage tracking, advertising pixels, remote fonts, or behavioral analytics. The language selector uses only the URL query parameter and your browser language.

3. How information is used

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

  • authenticate through the supported platform and operate the Game;
  • save local progress and settings;
  • deliver, verify, and restore purchased content;
  • respond to support requests and resolve technical problems;
  • protect the Game, prevent fraud or misuse, and comply with law; and
  • enforce this Policy and the Terms of Use.

Where a legal basis is required, processing is based as applicable on performance of our agreement, legitimate interests in operating and securing the Game, your consent, or compliance with legal obligations. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Third parties and sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Depending on your device and actions, information may be processed by:

  • Apple Inc. for Game Center, the App Store, in-app purchases, device backup, and platform services.
  • Google LLC. for Google Play Games, Google Play, in-app purchases, device backup, and platform services.
  • Unity Technologies. as the Game engine and the technical in-app-purchase integration layer.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, to protect rights or safety, with your consent, or as part of a lawful business transfer.

Provider policies: Apple, Google, Unity, and GitHub.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data remains on the device until it is reset, cleared, removed with the Game, or lost through a device change or reset.
  • Purchase entitlement information remains locally as needed to provide the purchase and may be restored from the store. Apple and Google retain transaction records under their own policies.
  • Platform account and backup data is retained by the relevant provider under its policies and your settings.
  • Public support issues remain on GitHub until removed or otherwise handled under GitHub’s retention practices.

The current release does not create a separate Disaster Afterward account or server-side player profile for us to delete. To remove local data, use the Game’s reset option if available, clear the app’s data in the operating system, or uninstall the Game. Uninstalling does not delete your Apple or Google account, transaction history, or platform backups; manage those through the relevant provider.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal data; object to certain processing; receive portable data; withdraw consent; or complain to a regulator. Because we currently hold no developer-operated player database, many requests concern information held by Apple, Google, or GitHub and should be directed to that provider.

To ask a privacy question or exercise a right concerning information we control, use the contact channel below. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request, and may retain limited records when required by law.

7. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature and risk of the information. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Apple, Google, Unity, and GitHub may process information outside your country or region under their policies and applicable transfer safeguards.

8. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed primarily to children who cannot lawfully consent to personal-data processing without parental authorization in their location. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information to us without required authorization may contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Game, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through this site, the Game, or another appropriate channel.
